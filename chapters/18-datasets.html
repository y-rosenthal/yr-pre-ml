The datasets#

The chapters use four data files. They are made up, but realistic: a script makes them with fixed random numbers, so they are the same for everyone. Some values are missing on purpose, because real data has missing values too.

Each chapter reads a file from the book’s GitHub repository:

import pandas as pd

DATA_URL = "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/y-rosenthal/yr-pre-ml/main/data/"
customers = pd.read_csv(DATA_URL + "customers.csv")

To use a file without internet (for example in Thonny), open its link below, save it next to your script, and read it by its name: pd.read_csv("customers.csv").

customers.csv#

customers.csv: 500 members of a store chain’s loyalty program, one row for each member.

Column

Meaning

Values

age

The member’s age, in years

18 to 75; 14 values are missing

region

The region of the member’s store (text)

North, South, East, West

months_as_customer

How long the person has been a member, in months

1 to 120

visits_per_month

The average number of store visits per month

0.6 to 12.0

avg_basket

The average amount spent per visit, in dollars

about 12 to 97; 11 values are missing

annual_spend

The amount spent last year, in dollars: the target

about 260 to 11,500

sales.csv#

sales.csv: 2,000 sales in the eight stores of a chain during one year, one row for each sale, in date order. No values are missing.

Column

Meaning

Values

date

The day of the sale (text, YYYY-MM-DD)

2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31

store

The store (text)

S1 to S8

region

The region of the store (text)

North (S1, S2), South (S3, S4), East (S5, S6), West (S7, S8)

category

The product category (text)

Grocery, Clothing, Home, Electronics

units

The number of units sold

1 to 16

price

The price of one unit before the discount, in dollars

1.99 to 597.99

discount

The discount, as a fraction of the price

0.0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2

The revenue of a sale is units * price * (1 - discount).

deliveries.csv#

deliveries.csv: 500 deliveries of a food-delivery company, one row for each delivery.

Column

Meaning

Values

distance_km

The distance from the restaurant to the customer, in kilometers

0.4 to 17.5

items

The number of items in the order

1 to 12

time_of_day

When the order was placed (text)

Morning, Afternoon, Evening

weather

The weather during the delivery (text)

Clear, Rain, Snow; 18 values are missing

driver_years

The driver’s experience, in years

0 to 15; 30 values are missing

minutes

How long the delivery took, in minutes: the target

about 6 to 75

marketing.csv#

marketing.csv: the weekly sales of one product in four regions, for 130 weeks (520 rows), with the advertising of each week.

Column

Meaning

Values

region

The sales region (text)

North, South, East, West (130 rows each)

tv_spend

Money spent on TV advertising that week, in thousands of dollars

0.1 to 50.0

online_spend

Money spent on online advertising that week, in thousands of dollars

0.0 to 19.9; 26 values are missing

price

The product’s price that week, in dollars

8.00 to 15.00

promotion

Whether a store promotion ran that week (text)

Yes, No; 12 values are missing

units_sold

The number of units sold that week: the target

about 100 to 810

How the files are made#

The script data/make_data.py in the book’s repository makes all four files. It is a good example of NumPy and pandas at work, once you have read the book.
